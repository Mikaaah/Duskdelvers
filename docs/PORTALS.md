# Player-built Tier Gateways — 0.4.0

There are five fully player-made expedition portals, no naturally generated hub, structure or entrance temple.

Each frame is **4 × 5 blocks**, with an empty **2 × 3** interior, oriented along X or Z:

~~~text
F F F F
F . . F
F . . F
F . . F
F F F F
~~~

| Gateway tier | Frame material |
| --- | --- |
| I | Mossy Stone Bricks |
| II | Prismarine Bricks |
| III | Amethyst Blocks |
| IV | Polished Blackstone Bricks |
| V | Crying Obsidian |

Right-click a frame block with a **Delver's Compass** to activate the frame; then walk inside. This begins a timed **open-world cave expedition**, not a generated dungeon.

The selected gateway tier sets your time allowance and a **preferred biome difficulty at your starting location**. It does not lock other biomes: you can explore any danger level. Team members enter together from near their leader.

To leave, use your Delver's Compass from inside the cave or \`/duskdelvers exit\`. The older return gateways placed in 0.2/0.3 dungeon rooms are not generated in new worlds. Expiry still extracts you automatically.

The compass can also start a tier directly by sneak-right-clicking outside the dimension to choose a tier, then right-clicking in the air to enter.
